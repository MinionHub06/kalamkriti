Our Story

From a small village in West Bengal to the world stage, we've been weaving dreams and preserving traditions for over six decades.

Our Journey Through Time

Six decades of dedication to handloom craftsmanship

1955 - The Beginning

Founded by Master Weaver Rajendra Kumar in Silainagar, West Bengal, with just two looms and a dream to preserve traditional weaving techniques.

1
2

1970s - Expansion

Expanded to 15 looms and trained 50+ local artisans. Introduced modern quality control while maintaining traditional techniques.

1990s - Digital Revolution

Embraced technology to reach global markets while maintaining authentic craftsmanship. Launched our first catalog.

3
4

2010s - Sustainability Focus

Pioneered sustainable practices, organic cotton sourcing, and fair trade partnerships. Launched our apprenticeship program.

2024 - Innovation Meets Tradition

Launched our digital platform, blockchain authenticity verification, and bespoke studio. Connecting artisans directly with conscious consumers worldwide.

5
