Meet Our Master Weavers

Behind every KalamKriti creation is a story of passion, tradition, and artistry. Meet the talented weavers who bring our vision to life.

Rajesh Kumar

Master Weaver • 25 years experience

Specializes in traditional Banarasi silk weaving. Rajesh has been perfecting his craft since age 12, learning from his father and grandfather.

Silainagar, West Bengal

Priya Sharma

Cotton Specialist • 18 years experience

Expert in organic cotton weaving and natural dyeing techniques. Priya is passionate about sustainable fashion and eco-friendly practices.

Kolkata, West Bengal

Amit Singh

Zari Work Master • 30 years experience

Renowned for intricate zari work and gold thread weaving. Amit's creations have been featured in fashion shows across India.

Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh

Sunita Devi

Block Print Expert • 22 years experience

Master of traditional block printing techniques. Sunita creates stunning patterns using age-old methods passed down through generations.

Jaipur, Rajasthan

Mohammed Ali

Pashmina Specialist • 28 years experience

Expert in Kashmir pashmina weaving. Mohammed creates the finest shawls and stoles using traditional techniques from the Kashmir Valley.

Srinagar, Kashmir

Lakshmi Bai

Chanderi Master • 35 years experience

Specialist in Chanderi silk weaving. Lakshmi creates lightweight, elegant fabrics perfect for modern fashion while maintaining traditional techniques.

Chanderi, Madhya Pradesh
